                #text "1. Introduction"
              </h2>
              <section class="cr-subsection" id="sms-rules-1-1">
                <h3>
                  #text "1.1 Purpose"
                </h3>
                <p>
                  #text "These rules establish a Competition Committee and a standardized competitive ruleset."
                </p>
              </section>
              <section class="cr-subsection" id="sms-rules-1-2">
                <h3>
                  #text "1.2 Scope and Rule Priority"
                </h3>
                <p>
                  #text "These rules apply by default to Mario Strikers League (MSL) competitions and community-organized tournaments."
                </p>
                <p>
                  #text "If a tournament publishes explicit event-specific rule changes, those rules apply only to that event."
                </p>
                <p>
                  #text "Where game-specific rules conflict with universal rules, game-specific rules take precedence."
                </p>
                <p>
                  #text "For unresolved disputes, Tournament Staff decisions are final for the current event."
                </p>
              </section>

              <h2>
                #text "2. Universal Competitive Rules"
              </h2>
              <section class="cr-subsection" id="sms-rules-2-1">
                <h3>
                  #text "2.1 Match Administration"
                </h3>
                <p>
                  #text "Before a set starts, players must confirm host setup, match settings, and any organizer requirements."
                </p>
                <p>
                  #text "If players cannot agree before the set starts, involve Tournament Staff before gameplay. If staff is unavailable, postpone the set until a ruling is possible."
                </p>
                <p>
                  #text "Once players explicitly agree before match start, the agreement is binding unless a material technical or competitive issue occurs."
                </p>
              </section>
              <section class="cr-subsection" id="sms-rules-2-2">
                <h3>
                  #text "2.2 Competitive Integrity"
                </h3>
                <p>
                  #text "All games must use organizer-approved settings and versions. Unapproved gameplay-altering methods are prohibited unless explicitly listed as legal in game-specific rules."
                </p>
                <p>
                  #text "Wired Ethernet is strongly recommended for the most stable competitive match quality and lower latency. It is not mandatory."
                </p>
                <p>
                  #text "Players should use a connection that avoids avoidable disruptions."
                </p>
                <p>
                  #text "Players must cooperate with staff requests for evidence, testing, and match-state reconstruction during disputes."
                </p>
                <p>
                  #text "Players must communicate clearly and act in good faith during setup, gameplay, and dispute resolution."
                </p>
              </section>
              <section class="cr-subsection" id="sms-rules-2-3">
                <h3>
                  #text "2.3 Dispute Escalation"
                </h3>
                <p>
                  #text "When a dispute occurs (for example stalling or integrity concerns), players must pause at the next legal point and request staff review."
                </p>
                <p>
                  #text "Provide video evidence whenever possible. If staff is unavailable, record exact details and suspend the set until review."
                </p>
                <p>
                  #text "Players should not continue play before official review is completed. If they do, retroactive staff action may be limited by available evidence and tournament policy."
                </p>
              </section>
            </div>

                #text "3. Game-Specific Rules & Settings"

                  #text "3.1 Game Version and Platform"

                <p>
                  #text "Download Citrus here: "

                    #text "Dolphin Download"

                </p>
              </section>
              <section class="cr-subsection" id="sms-rules-3-2">
                <h3>
                  #text "3.2 Dolphin Netplay Settings"
                </h3>
                <ul class="cr-list">
                  <li>
                    #text "Netplay lobby \"Network\" must be set to \"Fair Input Delay\"."
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    #text "Players should agree on a stable buffer and generally use the lowest value that avoids lag."
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    #text "A baseline formula is BUFFER = PING / 8, but values may be lower or higher if both players agree."
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    #text "The absolute minimum buffer is 6, and the absolute maximum buffer is 20."
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    #text "If no stable connection is possible at buffer 20 or lower, the match should be postponed."
                  </li>
                </ul>
              </section>
              <section class="cr-subsection" id="sms-rules-3-3">
                <h3>
                  #text "3.3 Match Settings and Visual Options"

                    #text "Skill Level: LEGEND"
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    #text "Match Time: 5 MINUTES"
                  </li>

                    #text "Power Ups: ON"
                  </li>
                  <li>

                <ul class="cr-list">

                    #text "Camera Type: AUTO ZOOM"

                    #text "Aspect Ratio: WIDE (16:9)"
                  </li>

              <section class="cr-subsection" id="sms-rules-3-4">
                <h3>
                  #text "3.4 Stadiums and Match Procedure"
                </h3>
                <p>
                  #text "Any stadium is allowed."
                </p>
                <p>
                  #text "This process applies to best-of-3, best-of-5, and best-of-7 formats:"
                </p>
                <ul class="cr-list">
                  <li>
                    #text "In bracket play, higher seed gets first choice. In round robin or Swiss, first choice is decided by coin flip."
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    #text "The winner of first choice selects one option: stadium, home/away side, or first captain pick."
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    #text "The other player selects one of the remaining two options, and the last option goes to the first player."
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    #text "Play the game with the selected stadium and side/captain setup."
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    #text "After each game, the loser chooses two options from stadium, home/away side, and captain, and the winner gets the remaining option."
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    #text "The player selecting stadium cannot choose a stadium they already won on earlier in the set unless both players agree."
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    #text "This restriction applies even when that prior stadium win happened on the opponent's stadium choice."
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    #text "If setup is incorrect (for example illegal stadium pick), players must quit within the first 5 seconds on the game timer; otherwise the game stands."
                  </li>
                </ul>
              </section>

            <div class="content-box-content">
              <h2>
                #text "4. Match Disruptions & Technical Issues"
              </h2>
              <section class="cr-subsection" id="sms-rules-4-1">
                <h3>
                  #text "4.1 Disconnections"
                </h3>
                <p>
                  #text "A disconnect may result in an automatic game loss, but if intent is unclear, match state should be reconstructed as accurately as possible."
                </p>
                <p>
                  #text "If players cannot agree on reconstruction details, staff should decide the official restart procedure."
                </p>
                <p>
                  #text "When reconstruction is approved, restore score, key state conditions, and possession as closely as possible."
                </p>
                <p>
                  #text "If a game-specific disconnection rule exists, that game-specific rule overrides this baseline."
                </p>
              </section>
              <section class="cr-subsection" id="sms-rules-4-2">
                <h3>
                  #text "4.2 Restarts and Misconfiguration"
                </h3>
                <p>
                  #text "Tournament organizers may approve restarts in exceptional circumstances (for example infrastructure or power interruptions)."
                </p>
                <p>
                  #text "If settings are misconfigured in a way that could affect the result, request review immediately after that game. Once the next game starts, the prior game is normally locked."
                </p>
                <p>
                  #text "Repeated misconfiguration may result in escalating penalties."
                </p>
              </section>
              <section class="cr-subsection" id="sms-rules-4-3">
                <h3>
                  #text "4.3 Stalling Review"
                </h3>
                <p>
                  #text "Stalling or excessive delay may result in game or set forfeiture at organizer discretion."
                </p>
                <p>
                  #text "If a player believes stalling occurred, formally call it, preserve evidence, and request staff review before continuing."
                </p>
                <p>
                  #text "Without sufficient evidence, staff action may be limited. If staff is unavailable, suspend the set and resume after review."
                </p>
              </section>
              <section class="cr-subsection" id="sms-rules-4-4">
                <h3>
                  #text "4.4 Pause Protocol"
                </h3>
                <p>
                  #text "Mid-game pauses are allowed only for urgent technical issues or real-life emergencies, preferably in non-contested states."
                </p>
                <p>
                  #text "Unauthorized pauses may result in automatic game forfeiture unless both players agree to a resolution or a true emergency is confirmed."
                </p>
              </section>
              <section class="cr-subsection" id="sms-rules-4-5">
                <h3>
                  #text "4.5 Penalty Ladder"
                </h3>
                <p>
                  #text "Rules violations are subject to penalties including, but not limited to:"
                </p>
                <ul class="cr-list">
                  <li>
                    #text "Game restart"
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    #text "Loss of a game"
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    #text "Loss of a match"
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    #text "Tournament forfeiture"
                  </li>
                </ul>
                <p>
                  #text "Any imposed penalty may be made public at organizer discretion."
                </p>
              </section>
              <section class="cr-subsection" id="sms-rules-4-6">
                <h3>
                  #text "4.6 Restart Reconstruction Details"
                </h3>
                <p>
                  #text "If players cannot agree after a disconnection, restart as close as possible to the original state:"
                </p>
                <ul class="cr-list">
                  <li>
                    #text "Score: Recreate score differential (for example crash at 7-4 -> recreate as 3-0)."
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    #text "Items: Recreate prior item state, recreate item differential, or agree to restart with no items."
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    #text "Time and possession: If one player had possession, that player walks the ball into goalie at the correct time and play continues. If possession was contested, walk to goalie and punt with B to resume neutral play."
                  </li>
                </ul>
              </section>
              <section class="cr-subsection" id="sms-rules-4-7">
                <h3>
                  #text "4.7 Multiple Crashes and Lag Disputes"
                </h3>
                <p>
                  #text "For repeated disconnections, reschedule the set after the issue is resolved. Tournament organizers may forfeit a game at their discretion."
                </p>
                <p>
                  #text "If connection quality is disputed, test in Strikers 101 before the first match and involve staff if no agreement is reached."
                </p>
              </section>
              <section class="cr-subsection" id="sms-rules-4-8">
                <h3>
                  #text "4.8 Stalling Examples"
                </h3>
                <p>
                  #text "Examples of illegal stalling tactics include:"
                </p>
                <ul class="cr-list">
                  <li>
                    #text "Standing still in front of your Kritter with the ball."
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    #text "Dribbling away from the opponent's goal with no defensive pressure and no offensive intent."
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    #text "Running kickoff possession back to your own defensive corner."
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    #text "Running back to your Kritter unless one of the following applies: Kritter is stunned/recovering, out of position, you are avoiding an incoming item, or you just collected the ball in your defensive corner."
                  </li>
                </ul>
                <p>
                  #text "Examples of legal time-wasting plays include:"
                </p>
                <ul class="cr-list">
                  <li>
                    #text "Holding possession with Kritter as long as possible."
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    #text "Clearing the ball downfield with a player or Kritter from any location, including kickoff."
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    #text "Guarding a loose ball by hitting opposing players attempting to challenge."
                  </li>
                </ul>
              </section>
              <section class="cr-subsection" id="sms-rules-4-9">
                <h3>
                  #text "4.9 Pause Timing Details"
                </h3>
                <p>
                  #text "In SMS, if a pause is necessary, wait until your own Kritter has full possession or immediately after a goal."
                </p>
                <p>
                  #text "Pauses should not exceed 1 minute unless active discussion with Tournament Staff is ongoing. If players cannot agree after 1 minute, contact staff immediately."
                </p>
              </section>

              <h2>
                #text "5. Scheduling & Matchmaking Conduct"
              </h2>
              <section class="cr-subsection" id="sms-rules-5-1">
                <h3>
                  #text "5.1 Scheduling Expectations"
                </h3>
                <p>
                  #text "Scheduling with your opponent is part of competitive play. Communicate early and often."
                </p>
                <ul class="cr-list">
                  <li>
                    #text "Players have real-life responsibilities; respect scheduled set times."
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    #text "If you cannot make a scheduled time, notify your opponent as early as possible."
                  </li>
                </ul>
              </section>
              <section class="cr-subsection" id="sms-rules-5-2">
                <h3>
                  #text "5.2 Delay and No-Show Penalties"
                </h3>
                <p>
                  #text "When players mutually agree on an official set time, late arrival or no-show without communication triggers these penalties:"
                </p>
                <ul class="cr-list">
                  <li>
                    #text "0-10 minutes late: No penalty"
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    #text "10-20 minutes late: Loss of 1 pick (opponent gets first two picks in Game 1 setup)"
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    #text "20-40 minutes late: Forfeit of Game 1"
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    #text "40-60 minutes late: Forfeit of Games 1 and 2"
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    #text "More than 60 minutes late or no-show: Forfeit of the set"
                  </li>
                </ul>
              </section>
              <section class="cr-subsection" id="sms-rules-5-3">
                <h3>
                  #text "5.3 Emergency Clause"
                </h3>
                <p>
                  #text "Tournament Staff may rescind penalties and allow rescheduling for valid emergencies that prevented prior communication."
                </p>
                <p>
                  #text "Abuse of this clause is prohibited. Repeated emergency claims may be denied at staff discretion."
                </p>
              </section>
              <section class="cr-subsection" id="sms-rules-5-4">
                <h3>
                  #text "5.4 Pressuring"
                </h3>
                <p>
                  #text "Set times must be mutually agreed. Short-notice play is allowed only by mutual agreement."
                </p>
                <p>
                  #text "Players must not pressure opponents into playing. Staff may penalize pressuring behavior."
                </p>
                <p>
                  #text "Examples include:"
                </p>
                <ul class="cr-list">
                  <li>
                    #text "Excessive pinging."
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    #text "Demanding immediate play by claiming your schedule cannot otherwise meet the deadline."
                  </li>
                </ul>
              </section>

              </h2>
              <section class="cr-subsection" id="sms-rules-6-1">
                <h3>
                  #text "6.1 Behavior Standards"
