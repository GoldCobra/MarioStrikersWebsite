The Mario Strikers: Battle League (Football) Competitive Rulebook

Last revised July 6, 2023

1. Introduction

These rules establish a Competition Committee and define a standardized competitive ruleset.

2. Gameplay Settings

2.1 Match Settings

The following match settings must be used in competitive tournaments throughout the Mario Strikers community.

  • Match Length: 4:00
  • Items: COLORED ? BLOCKS ONLY
  • Hyper Strikes: OFF
  • Gear: ALLOWED

2.2 Match Procedure

  1. The first listed player in a match acts as host (in 2v2, the team captain) and creates the lobby with official settings.
  2. After all players join, the first listed player or team chooses home side, and the second listed player or team chooses away side.
  3. Play the first game of the match.
  4. After Game 1, all players select "Rematch." Players must switch the side they defend for the next game.
  5. Play the next game.
  6. Repeat steps 4 and 5 until the match is complete according to the applicable best-of format.
  7. If a game is tied after the 2-minute golden-goal period, play an immediate deciding game where the first goal wins.

3. Prohibited Techniques and Selections

3.1 Camping and Stalling

Stalling or excessive delay may result in game or set forfeiture at the discretion of tournament organizers. Stalling includes possession play that burns time without counterplay or offensive intent.

Two explicit illegal stalling tactics are:

  • Running the ball back to your keeper, unforced, directly off kickoff.
  • Standing still in front of your goalie with possession.

These tactics are treated as non-interactive and may lead to set forfeiture. Variants and unknown stalling patterns may be reviewed by organizers case by case.

Legal tactics include running the ball back to your goalie under pressure and clearing the ball off kickoff.

3.1.1 Stalling Disputes

If a player or viewer alleges stalling, video evidence or a credible third-party account is required to proceed with a ruling. Without evidence, the game result stands unless both players mutually agree otherwise.

If evidence confirms stalling and a player intentionally misrepresented the situation, harsher penalties may apply.

3.2 Embroider's Chip

Embroider's Chip is banned out of caution. The technique involves chipping off the wall around midfield and shooting shortly after, often forcing Boom Boom out of position and creating non-competitive scoring situations.

3.3 Custom Firmware and Emulators

Playing tournament matches via custom firmware (CFW) or emulators is prohibited and may result in exclusion from tournaments organized by the Mario Strikers community.

4. Match Disruptions

4.1 Disconnections

If a disconnect occurs within the first 30 seconds, restart the game. If the first disconnect occurs after 30 seconds, start a new game and recreate the exact score and time using screenshot or clip evidence.

  • The player who had possession at disconnect regains possession when play resumes.
  • If possession was neutral, possession is decided by coin flip on resumption.
  • Neither player may hold items when the game resumes.
  • If a player disconnects a second time in the same match, that player forfeits the current game and all subsequent games in that match.

4.2 Lags

If a game is lagging, complete the game first. The result may be invalidated and replayed after review. If a player abandons that game, that player forfeits it.

  • Both players should provide lag evidence clips if possible.
  • Both players must run a Nintendo Switch connection test and provide screenshots.
  • MSL Staff may run additional connection checks to identify the likely source.
  • If no source can be identified, the match must continue to completion. In unresolved cases, MSL Staff may decide by coin flip.

4.3 Restarts

Tournament organizers may approve a restart under exceptional circumstances, such as a power outage.

4.4 Game Disruptions

If game settings are misconfigured in a way that could materially affect the result, the game must be replayed. If the host fails to set correct settings again, that host forfeits the current game.

4.5 Penalties

Rules violations are subject to penalties including, but not limited to:

  • Game restart
  • Loss of a game
  • Loss of a match
  • Tournament forfeiture
  • Exclusion from future tournaments

Any penalty imposed on a player may be made public at organizer discretion.

5. Code of Conduct

All players are expected to exhibit good sportsmanship and maintain respect for one another and for all tournament organizers. Players are expected to play at their best at all times and avoid behavior inconsistent with good sportsmanship, honesty, and fair play. Behavior considered inappropriate includes, but is not limited to:

  • Interfering with tournament operation
  • Acting in an unsportsmanlike or disruptive manner, or annoying, abusing, threatening, or harassing others
  • Engaging in collusion (for example any agreement to predetermine outcomes)
  • Cheating by any means
  • Intentionally delaying or tampering with gameplay
  • Offensive, vulgar, or obscene gamertags
  • Sexism, ageism, racism, or any other form of prejudice or bigotry
  • Engaging in violence or behavior deemed immoral, unethical, disgraceful, or indecent by tournament owners
  • Offering gifts or rewards to gain competitive advantage or create competitive disadvantage for opponents
  • Otherwise violating these rules
